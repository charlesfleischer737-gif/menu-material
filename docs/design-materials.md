# Restaurant template materials

Created for the premium Post Maker and Menus collection using the built-in image generation tool. The artwork contains no food, restaurant identity, lettering, or logos. Customer dish photography is composited separately and retains its original pixels.

## Final assets

- `public/design-materials/copper-velvet.webp` — original 1122 × 1402 artwork, converted to WebP at quality 88.
- `public/design-materials/botanical-paper.webp` — original 1122 × 1402 artwork, converted to WebP at quality 88.

## Generation briefs

**Copper velvet:** Original premium restaurant background material. Dark oxblood and espresso, restrained copper caustic light, subtle fine film grain. Quiet negative space in the upper and left areas, warm illumination at the lower right. Vertical 4:5 flat artwork. No text, logos, food, plates, product subjects, borders, or mockups.

**Botanical paper:** Original premium restaurant background material. Warm ivory cotton-rag paper with subtle blind-embossed botanical stems confined to the outer edges. Natural raking light and a soft olive-leaf shadow. Keep the central reading area clean. Vertical 4:5 flat artwork. No text, logos, food, plates, product subjects, borders, or mockups.

## Composition and references

The original artwork provides material and light; it is not a replacement for the restaurant's food photograph. Brand-colored gradients, restrained shadows, feathered transitions, and type are composed at export time. Menu names, descriptions, and prices remain searchable PDF text.

Visual research included [COQODAQ](https://www.coqodaq.com/menus), [Scarfes Bar](https://scarfesbar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Heroes-and-Villains.pdf), [53 by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/53), and [Bottega by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/bottega). Their artwork and identities were not copied or included in the product.
